Read-only integration for Google Antigravity CLI (agy) session history.

Reads metadata and FTS5 search indexes from ~/.gemini/antigravity-cli/conversation_summaries.db in WAL-safe read-only mode, and parses structured conversation transcripts from ~/.gemini/antigravity-cli/brain/<conversation-id>/.system_generated/logs/transcript_full.jsonl (falling back to transcript.jsonl).
